## 1. Psychological direction and spoiler discipline (W05/W13)
Build the experience around curiosity, attachment, doubt and dread. Explore fictional stalking, paranoia, obsessive behavior, privacy invasion, manipulation and the moral ambiguity of investigating another person's private life. Make the player able to question whether they are investigating, crossing boundaries, or being manipulated. The possibility of Nora being stalked and the possibility of player complicity are approved creative directions, not confirmed plot facts. Do not settle or disclose the actual explanation in creator-facing summaries. Preserve established canon, authored causality, clues, character knowledge and progression gates. Keep the actual timeline, source of anomalies, narrative resolutions and endings in developer-only story documents. Do not add real surveillance, real device tracking, actual account access, camera/microphone capture, or real-world stalking functionality.

## 2. Nora as a person (W02/W05/W06)
Expand ordinary late-night life, insomnia/late hours, work and personal routines, humor, friends, romantic feelings and adult relationships, uncertainty, affectionate exchanges, regrets, drafts and unsent messages, ordinary photos and private non-explicit romantic portraits. Give her agency, boundaries, believable moods and motivations; do not reduce her to a collection of clues or voyeuristic rewards. Maintain consistent appearance, locations, chronology and photo provenance. Not every photo or conversation is evidence. For sensitive gallery items use existing owner-authentication and authored recovery gates, spoiler-safe thumbnails/notification previews, optional concealment of private media, and no explicit imagery. Never frame a privacy violation as a reward. Creator approved the two recent romantic/private portrait concepts and the later late-night photo concepts in chat; binary intake, individual file inventory, continuity review (including inconsistent accessories/tattoos), dimensions, hashes, and actual bundle integration remain OPEN. Do not treat contact sheets or accidental icon variants as individual approved final photos; preserve originals and separate any derived crops. Do not imply the generated scenes are canon until verified and authored.

## 3. Uncertainty and evidence (W05/W13)
Approved possible techniques: unexplained photo arrivals, inconsistent timestamps, unsettling but authored notifications, unsent drafts, ambiguous old versus new records, and fictional characters reacting to actual player choices/questions. Use stable record IDs, source and provenance fields (including explicitly unknown sources), creation/import/display times, and a developer-only causal timeline. Do not make contradictions accidental; intentional uncertainty should remain internally testable. Preserve meaningful alternative interpretations without prematurely confirming a perpetrator or revealing a twist. No random horror cue on routine alerts, no real-world surveillance methods. Ensure essential evidence remains recoverable under accessibility settings and notification dismissal.

## 4. Living notification layer (W01/W03/W06/W09/W12/W13)
Build a shared local event scheduler with stable event IDs, fictional app source, event kind, authored due time, delivery time, read/dismiss state, preview privacy, route, category and optional evidence link. Persist pending and delivered IDs across app switches, force-close, saves/restores and simulated restart; reconcile checkpoint rollbacks deliberately. Integrate with existing sensory engine, respect silent mode, sound/haptic/horror preferences and per-app notification settings. No actual system notifications without separately approved opt-in. A believable in-game notification center, lock-screen cards, banners, app icons, timestamps, unread badges and history; grouping, dismiss/read, tap-to-open correct permitted destination, private-preview redaction and accessibility. Natural reply delays, typing/read indicators, occasional ordinary activity, quiet periods and late-night rhythm; no universal instant replies, no flooding, no duplicate delivery on open/read/restore, no real-time dependency that blocks progress. Authored anomalies must have an explicit trigger, gating, bounded repeat behavior and a private explanation; routine alerts remain normal. Distinguish incoming call, missed call, voicemail, mail, social, timer, recovery and security cues. Reuse the approved phone effects where appropriate; additional sound assets require intake and verification.

Sources: Messages (replies/read/typing as appropriate); Mail (genuine new replies); Phone (fictional missed calls and voicemail); Afterframe (photo comments/reactions/DMs); Connective (professional updates); CommonGround (discussion replies); Dayblink (private photo/video messages and expiry); Motionwell (creator uploads, replies and subscriptions); LumaOS (security and recovery activity); Clock (timer). Notification history must remain truthful to the authored state and not manufacture evidence. Prefer ordinary contextual timing rather than triggering everything when an app opens.
