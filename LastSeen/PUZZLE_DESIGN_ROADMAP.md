## Proposed original puzzle families (initial scope)
1. Service routing: interactive invented module graph, connect compatible labeled ports and constraints to restore a fictional service. No actual networking or exploitation. Touch select/connect alternative to drag.
2. Integrity reconstruction: reconstruct local record fragments using internal consistency and metadata, not repeated arbitrary word scrambles; text and visual alternatives. Use only legitimately visible material.
3. Signal alignment: match abstract signal shapes/timing with a reference, adjustable controls; equivalent visual/text input independent of hearing, no required microphone. No rapid reaction or precision timing requirement.
4. Configuration diagnosis: inspect an invented status panel, compare observations with a local reference, identify conflicting setting and safely correct it; reversible sandbox, no real commands or device settings.
5. Timeline reconciliation: compare already discovered fictional messages/calendar/file records, arrange event relationships and identify contradictions based on evidence, allow uncertainty and equivalent valid order where chronology is partial.
6. Record authentication: match relevant provenance/consistency indicators across permitted records and explain which record is better supported; do not imply that real signatures are broken or teach credential bypass.
7. Repository investigation: use legitimately discovered local evidence to identify an authorized fictional source and add it through Relay. This complements the source-discovery milestone, not blind guessing or real network lookup.
Choose a few meaningful playable instances of the strongest families first (suggested 4–6 new instances), then extend based on testing. No mandatory identical repetitions; no forced trivia, real-time grinding or unrelated arcade games. Later cross-app mysteries can combine mechanics where narratively earned; avoid blocking the entire investigation behind optional cosmetic certifications. Final authored contexts, solution logic and rewards remain private and spoiler-marked.

No real device modifications, network, downloaded executable content or external services. Clearly state what the player must achieve and how to interact. Add plain-language Explain Procedure, opt-in hints in three stages (rephrase objective, point toward currently available evidence, explain approach without immediately revealing solution), contextual feedback on errors, and eventual Luma Assistant explanation hooks. Preserve original technical atmosphere. No automatic hints that reveal an answer, avoid penalties for asking. Premium LumaOS design, accessible alternatives for drag, color, audio, motor precision, readable text, VoiceOver and reduced motion. Meaningful rewards and notification records.
