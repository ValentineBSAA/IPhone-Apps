# POTS Tracker v0.1.5: Quiet status dots and check-in notes

## Based on real iPhone 16e screenshots
v0.1.4 grouped History successfully showed date → Check-ins → individual records. Home's recent activity remained buried after shortcuts, creating bulky repeated entry cards. One-tap feeling buttons saved repeated one-word entries without any context.

## Implemented
- Four status dots appear near top of Home: Feeling, Symptoms, Heart rate and BP. Filled teal indicates at least one record today; hollow gray indicates not recorded. Dots do not imply normal, abnormal, severity or health state.
- Tapping a dot opens the corresponding entry form. History remains the place for all individual records.
- Replaced the former Recent Activity module with a short daily status row; retained its persisted Home module ID to preserve custom preferences. Unchanged old default orders migrate to Today → Dots → Check-in → Shortcuts; custom orders remain intact.
- The latest entry appears as one line only when relevant, not three repeated cards.
- Tapping Better / Usual / Worse / Much worse / Not sure opens a check-in note editor. Feeling is preselected, title suggested but editable, notes optional, time adjustable, and nothing is saved until Save.
- Edit preserves feeling, custom title and notes. History displays titles and a short feeling-plus-note description.
- No Core Data migration: the existing checkIn title is freeform, notes are details, and the posture field stores selected feeling only for checkIn category. Older title-only check-ins are recognized automatically; HR/BP posture keeps its original meaning.
- Preserve the 13 approved icons, four tabs, safe delete confirmation, swipeable grouped History, iOS 16, local storage, no Apple Watch, no accounts and no paywalls.
