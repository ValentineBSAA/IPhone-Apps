## Research and limitations
Contact PPG can estimate resting pulse from fluctuations in camera-measured fingertip light, but accuracy varies with hand motion, pressure, poor perfusion, skin tone, ambient light, camera, pulse irregularity and rapid rate changes. Beta is **not validated for POTS**, diagnosis, emergency monitoring or treatment. A trustworthy external monitor should be used to verify estimates.
