## What this particular icon means
Berry Story is a **chapter-based game mode**, not a cottage building. The existing Home card resolves to `BerryStory`; chapter progress is presented separately as `Chapter N Ready`. Preserve the mode identity and actual chapter/progression logic. Use a clearly legible **storybook with open illustrated pages**, a strawberry bookmark and chapter stars, as the central symbol. Each holiday decorates the book without hiding its meaning. **Never bake a specific chapter number, words, fictional button labels, fixed time or reward amount into pixels.**

- One separate full-resolution illustration at a time, visibly presented for explicit owner approval.
- Same premium, glossy, berry-bakery rendering as prior approved Night/Seasonal Garden artwork; distinct Day/Night palette, not a flat recolor.
- Safe compact two-column Home tile crop (square-ish primary focal area, ample margins), bold open-book silhouette, readable at iPhone size.
- No borrowed recognizable commercial characters, text, watermark, stock imagery or reused Story tiles outside this family.
- Original source PNG is immutable; only after approval create **separate RGBA candidates**, verify real transparency rather than baked checkerboard, and preview on light/dark/checkerboard plus 64/96px crop.
- Images belong in `StrawberryShortcakeBlockBlast/ArtSource/SeasonalGarden/<Holiday>/<Day|Night>/Originals/Season_<Holiday>_<Day|Night>_BerryStory_Original.png`, separate from `ClockFrames/` and original approved generic `NightGarden/`.
- All existing gameplay, chapter rewards, saved runs, quest status, generic Day art, Night originals, base/holiday clocks and version/build remain untouched. No Xcode import before separate integration approval.
