# Source / license checklist

The 16 MP3 effects were provided in the user's `Berry Blast Sound Effects.zip`, reported as newly generated using ElevenLabs. This package converts the audio to WAV and organizes it by event. No trimming or montage occurred.

Before distributing in a public game, confirm the ElevenLabs subscription and asset rights that applied at generation time. Avoid third-party brand, character, or copyrighted recording claims unless separately licensed. Sound-effect licensing and source-code licensing should be evaluated separately.
