# Source / license checklist

The 51 original input files came from the user's uploaded archive `51 Berry Soundtrack.zip`. This package reorganizes and **transcodes** them; it does not create new compositions or alter the arrangement.

The user reports generating the music in Suno under a Premier subscription. Before public distribution, preserve Suno song links and creation dates, verify their generation-time commercial entitlements, and ensure no third-party protected melodies, lyrics, cover recordings, brand assets, or character IP are used without permission. A Suno commercial license does not grant rights to the Strawberry Shortcake franchise.

Do not assume the music is public domain or assign a blanket open-source audio license. Repo source-code licensing and audio licensing should be handled separately.
